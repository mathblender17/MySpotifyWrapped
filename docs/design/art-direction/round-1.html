A · Ukiyo-e × Gyaru zine

Real Hiroshige Tōkaidō prints (public domain, 1830s) are the backgrounds. Each station is a stop on Japan's original road trip. Gyaru stickers and kaomoji get slapped over them, with a print cartouche, a hanko stamp and paper grain.

Motion: stickers slap on, stamps thunk, paper flutters. 3D world: layered paper-cut scenery in the prints' indigo and vermilion.

B · Neon riso night

Real Tokyo night photos duotoned in two fluorescent inks with a halftone, and huge condensed numbers printed slightly off-register, like a riso zine. The neon-sign kanji carry the station name.

Motion: signs flicker on, inks slide into register, numbers roll like a departure board. 3D world: rainy night, neon glow, wet reflections.

C · Mega type

Closest to Wrapped's own language: whole fields of flat colour, enormous cropped type as the picture, one big shape (the rising sun), and kinetic katakana strips. No photos, just bold and graphic.

Motion: words slam in, split and slide; the band swings in on the beat. 3D world: flat-colour abstract Japan with big simple shapes.
