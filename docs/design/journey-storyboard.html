How it all connects: one journey through one year in Japan

Spoiler: 45 different art styles don't hang together just because they're placed in a row. They hang together because one ride runs through all of them. Here's what Wrapped does, the 5 threads I'd use to tie our slides together, and the full storyboard.

What Wrapped does to never lose the plot

🎵 Your music plays throughoutEach section plays a snippet of a song from your stats, which crossfades into the next. This is the strongest glue there is: it's your soundtrack.
🪝 Hook, then revealEvery section opens with a teaser ("You had a lot of favorites, but one stood out…") before the number lands. Anticipation, then payoff.
🎨 One system per yearOne palette, one type family and one shape language that bends per slide. Each slide differs, but they're clearly siblings.
📚 Layers in depthThe 2018 build put each slide's layers on separate Z-planes, so moving between slides creates parallax: you feel like you're travelling, not flipping pages.
⏸ Rhythm and breathersBig reveals alternate with light moments, quizzes and pauses, so the heavy numbers never pile up.
▬ Story progress barSegments at the top always show where you are and how much is left.

Our 5 connecting threads

① The train line
路線図 · THE SPINE
The progress bar is a train route map. The 13 slides are 13 stations, so you always see which stop you're at. The 3D camera literally rides the rails between districts.
② The seasons
四季 · THE PLOT
The ride runs from spring to New Year's midnight, like your year. The light, sky and weather in the 3D world shift continuously from sakura, to summer heat, to autumn leaves, to snow, to the final bell. That's the plot.
③ The gyaru guide
案内人 · THE VOICE
Your guide is a gyaru who texts you on a flip phone. She pops in between stations with the hook ("ぇ、次ゃばぃょ…") and sometimes hijacks a slide mid-way. Same character, same voice, every stop.
④ Your soundtrack
BGM · THE FEELING
Every station plays a 30-second preview of the song the stat is about (via the free iTunes preview API). It crossfades through the ride, so you hear your year as you move through it.
⑤ The ticket
切符 · THE BATON
You carry a paper train ticket, and each station stamps it (eki stamp). By the end it's covered in 13 stamps, and that stamped ticket becomes your share card next to the Kanji of the Year.
