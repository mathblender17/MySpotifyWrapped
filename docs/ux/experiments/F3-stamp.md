# F3 · Stamp punch

**Action:** press and hold on the ticket for about half a second, then let go. A quick tap is only a poke.

**Response:** while held, a ring fills, the ticket sinks slightly and the phone buzz builds up. On release the stamp slams down (a hard hit, a pause, a small rebound), ink splats outward, the screen shakes a few pixels, and the kanji 乗 stays on the ticket.

**Feeling:** you really pressed a stamp onto paper.

**Data:** none. Demo ticket only.

**Cost:** one GSAP tween while holding, eight short-lived elements on release. Sound and vibration only after the first tap. Android vibrates; iPhone gets sound and motion. Under reduced motion a tap just stamps.

**Kill if:** the hold feels like waiting rather than building, or the slam feels weak.
