# F2 · Tilt world

**Action:** tilt the phone left, right, forward or back. On iPhone, tap "Enable tilt" once (iOS asks for permission). Without tilt, drag a finger across the scene.

**Response:** four paper-cut layers (night sky, Fuji, hills with a torii, the train) slide at different speeds, so the flat art gains real depth.

**Feeling:** the scene is a diorama you hold in your hand.

**Data:** none. Demo art only.

**Cost:** four CSS transforms per sensor reading, smoothed. The sensor runs only while the experiment is open. Off under reduced motion. Denied or missing sensor falls back to the finger.

**Kill if:** it feels seasick or the effect is too small to notice.
