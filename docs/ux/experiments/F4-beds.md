# F4 · Sound beds

**Action:** tap one of three worlds (torii, konbini, gacha). Tap it again to stop. Tapping another switches.

**Response:** a short ambience loops: a slow wind for the torii, a three-note door chime every few seconds for the konbini, a ratchet then a pause for the gacha. The pressed button's level bars dance.

**Feeling:** each world has a place, not just a picture.

**Data:** none. Demo only.

**Cost:** one gain-and-filter chain (wind) or a few short oscillators on a timer (chime, ratchet). No audio files. Sound starts after the first tap and is silent under reduced motion or mute. Every bed peaks at 0.08, under the ride's −18 dB ceiling.

**Kill if:** it sounds like noise rather than a place, or it fights the song mix.
